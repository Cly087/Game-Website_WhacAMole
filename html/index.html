Hit the Moles!

Those messy little pests are appearing all over the place! Hit the mole as soon as it appears from its hole. Act fast, since they enjoy dashing back down just before your hammer hits. The higher number of moles you knock out, the greater your score will become!

Avoid the Bombs!

Be careful about the sneaky moles who have bombs! They're exactly like the rest of them, only they've got a nasty surprise. If you hit one, then it goes off and you lose points! Identify the one with the bomb, keep your hammer ready, and let those troublemakers waddle back down.

Watch Out for Strikes!

Every mole that gets away earns you a strike, and so does hitting a bomb, so choose your targets wisely! Collect too many strikes, and it's game over. Stay focused, stay speedy, and keep those moles in check while steering clear of the bombs!
